# House style — delivery reporting

## Rules

1. **Every claim carries a source.** If it is not in a source document, it does not go in the
   report. If it matters and no source covers it, say so in section 5 as a gap.
2. **Dates are absolute.** "2026-09-30", never "end of next month".
3. **Named owners.** Every milestone and risk has a person. "The team" is not an owner; if
   nothing else, write "unassigned" — that is information.
4. **Status is one of:** Not started, In progress, At risk, Complete, Cancelled. Nothing else.
5. **Slips are stated as a movement,** with both dates: "2026-09-15 → 2026-09-30".
6. **No adjectives about progress.** Not "good progress" or "solid month". State what moved.
7. **The summary is written last** and says what the reader should do.

## Tone

Written for a director who has six of these to read. Short sentences. No preamble, no
restating the template's own headings back at the reader.
