# Proposal — CASE-4471, Brightmoor Health

**Classification:** Configuration
**Confidence:** high · **Risk:** low · **Reversible:** yes

## What is wrong

Three of Brightmoor Health's users sign in on the domain `brightmoor-health.org`. Only
`brightmoorhealth.com` is registered for SSO on account `acc-1042`. A login domain that is
not registered falls back to password login, and there is no wildcard matching — so those
users are sent to the password page while everybody on the registered domain is unaffected.

That matches the customer's own description exactly: a subset of staff, unchanged behaviour
for the rest, and nothing changed on their side. The second domain was never registered.

## Why this is not the other three classifications

**Not a product defect.** The platform did what the documentation says it does. A defect
requires the platform to have done something other than what it promised — here it refused a
login for a domain nobody registered, which is the documented behaviour.

**Not user error.** The affected users did nothing wrong; the account is missing a
registration. Calling this user error would put the fault on the people who cannot log in.

**Not need-more-information.** The deciding facts are all present: the domains, the users and
the audit trail agree.

## Proposed resolution

Register `brightmoor-health.org` as a second SSO domain on `acc-1042`, then have one affected
user confirm. Multi-domain SSO is included on Enterprise, which this account is on, so no
plan change is needed.

**This proposal publishes the triage note only.** Registering the domain is a change to the
customer's account and is not among the actions here — it needs its own approval, from
somebody who can make account changes.

## Rollback

Delete `triage/CASE-4471.md`. Nothing is sent to the customer and no account is modified by
applying this proposal, so there is nothing else to undo.

## What I could not determine

- Whether the clinics side was always on a second domain, or migrated recently. The audit
  log only goes back 30 days and the first denial is inside that window.
- Whether other accounts have the same unregistered-domain problem and have not reported it.
  Answering that needs a query across every account's domains, which is outside this case.
