# Customer communications policy

Last reviewed: 2026-07-11. Owner: Support leadership.

What we say, and when, once a ticket has been classified.

## Configuration

Say what was misconfigured, that we have fixed it or how they can, and what to do if it
recurs. **Do not describe it as a fault on their side** — most configuration traps are
behaviours that are not obvious from the interface.

## User error

Name the limitation, give the workaround, and route the commercial conversation to the
account manager. Never send "you are not entitled to this" without a workaround or a route.

## Product defect

Say that we have reproduced it, that it is with the platform vendor, and what the workaround
is. **Do not give a fix date** unless the vendor has committed one in writing; a date we
invented becomes a promise we break.

## Need more information

Ask for the specific missing facts and say why each one is needed. A bare "can you provide
more detail" gets a bare reply.

## What never goes to a customer without approval

- A remediation applied to their account
- Any statement about a fix date
- Any acknowledgement that a defect affects other customers

The last one matters: confirming that other accounts are affected is a disclosure decision,
not a support decision.
